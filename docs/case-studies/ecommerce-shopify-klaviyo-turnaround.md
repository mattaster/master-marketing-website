Ecommerce Turnaround — Shopify & Klaviyo

Overview

This case study describes how I diagnosed gaps in an ecommerce client's strategy, prioritized fixes, and executed a focused turnaround using Shopify and Klaviyo. The client was an SMB ecommerce store with rising ad spend but falling conversion and underperforming email revenue.

Challenges

- Incomplete and inconsistent event tracking between Shopify and analytics.
- Klaviyo lists and flows were poorly segmented and relied on outdated properties.
- Checkout friction in Shopify causing cart abandonment and lost attribution.
- Marketing team lacked a clear playbook and engineers were unsure which fixes were highest priority.

Approach

1. Rapid discovery (30–60 minute call + access review).
2. Systems audit: Shopify storefront, checkout settings, Klaviyo integration, and analytics.
3. Prioritized triage: list top 6 issues, then focus on the top 3 for a 2-week sprint.
4. Hands-on fixes + team handoff: implement changes, validate results, and document playbook.

Implementation — Shopify

- Fixed checkout settings and simplified cart scripts causing slow page loads.
- Validated Shopify’s purchase event payloads and ensured order/money values matched Klaviyo and analytics.
- Standardized product and variant tagging to enable reliable attribution.
- Added server-side order webhooks where necessary to guarantee event delivery.

Implementation — Klaviyo

- Repaired the Shopify-Klaviyo sync so lists, profiles, and events were consistent.
- Segmented customers by behavior and LTV; rebuilt high-impact flows: abandoned cart, browse abandonment, and welcome series.
- Implemented event-triggered emails using the corrected purchase and checkout events.
- Cleaned up profile properties to enable accurate personalization and suppression rules.

Team Enablement & Playbook

- Delivered a 1-page triage with the top 3 fixes and estimated effort (low/med/high).
- Ran a 2-hour coaching session with marketing and engineering to explain changes and how to validate metrics.
- Supplied a short-runbook for monitoring Klaviyo flow performance and Shopify event integrity.

Results (typical outcomes)

- Restored accurate event attribution across Shopify, Klaviyo, and analytics.
- Abandoned-cart recovery revenue increased (example: +20% in flow revenue month-over-month after fixes).
- Conversion rate improved after checkout simplifications (example: +10–18% over baseline in A/B testing).
- Marketing regained confidence and had a reproducible process for future experiments.

Key Takeaways

- Small technical inconsistencies can dramatically distort performance signals — fix tracking first.
- Prioritize fixes that restore reliable data and carve out quick wins (flows, checkout friction) before major re-architecture.
- Pair fixes with team training so improvements persist after the engagement ends.
